## Turning a radio website into a lively local hub.

The Enjoy 33 website was redesigned to create a dynamic and welcoming experience. Clean navigation, bold pink branding and a direct content hierarchy make the station accessible to new listeners while keeping the personality its existing community already knows.

### Listening without friction

The central radio player delivers continuous live streaming across devices. Its minimal interface keeps the station one click away, while careful loading and caching choices support reliable playback in modern browsers.

### A connected community

An automatically updated Instagram feed, powered by the Meta API, brings events, studio moments and recent highlights directly into the site. Visitors can stay connected to the station’s activity without leaving the experience.

The partner directory gives local businesses, associations and organisations a dedicated place to be discovered. Filterable profiles gather useful descriptions and contact details, strengthening the connection between the audience and the regional network.

Together, live audio, social content and local discovery make the website more than a player: it becomes a digital meeting point for listeners and partners.
