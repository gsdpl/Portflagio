## Transforming time on the road into a shared experience.

Naviroll is a mobile product concept designed to improve long coach journeys. It combines an exclusive entertainment library with useful travel updates and lightweight social features, giving passengers more engaging ways to spend their time.

### Entertainment for the journey

Passengers can discover animations, short films and interactive stories selected for different audiences. Some narratives invite users to make choices, turning passive viewing into a more participatory experience and creating natural topics for conversation.

### Useful and social

A built-in chat allows passengers to discuss what they are watching and connect during the trip. Journey notifications provide practical updates such as arrival information or possible delays, reducing uncertainty without interrupting the entertainment experience.

Designed in Figma and prototyped with FlutterFlow, Naviroll brings content, communication and travel information into one coherent mobile interface.
