## An intelligent assistant for radio sales teams.

Commbot is a conversational assistant built for the sales team of a radio advertising agency. It centralises business knowledge and streamlines client meeting preparation through a multimodal RAG architecture.

### Architecture and ingestion

The system ingests diverse sources — PDF documents, WordPress pages, images and videos — to build a rich, up-to-date knowledge base. Hybrid search combines semantic and full-text approaches, with French-specific linguistic normalisation and multilingual embedding models.

### Meeting preparation

The flagship feature lets sales representatives prepare client meetings: the assistant automatically aggregates relevant information about an advertiser — history, industry, tailored offers — to generate a structured, source-backed briefing document.

### Technical stack

The product is built with **Python** and **FastAPI** for the API layer, **Supabase** with pgvector for vector storage, the **Claude API** (Anthropic) for contextualised response generation, and **Coolify** for self-hosted deployment.
